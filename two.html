Ready to dive into an unforgettable underwater experience? Book your diving adventure with us today and explore the best diving spots across Canada. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced diver, we have something for everyone.


Booking Process

Our booking process is simple and straightforward. Follow these steps to secure your spot for an unforgettable diving experience:

  1. Choose Your Destination: Explore our top diving destinations across Canada and select the one that suits your preferences.

  2. Select Your Package: Choose from our range of diving packages, including guided dives, training courses, all-inclusive packages, and custom experiences.

    Guided Dive

    Explore top dive sites with experienced local guides who ensure safety while helping you discover the best underwater features. Perfect for both beginners and certified divers looking for a structured and enjoyable dive experience.

    $120/hour per person

    3-hour Training Course

    Learn essential diving skills through structured lessons led by certified instructors. Ideal for beginners or those looking to advance their certification in a supportive group environment.

    Price: $60 per person (group class of 10)

    3-Day Immersive All-Inclusive Package

    Experience the ultimate diving getaway with a fully planned 3-day adventure. This package includes guided dives at top locations, accommodation, meals, equipment, and transportation, allowing you to focus entirely on exploring Canada’s stunning underwater world. Perfect for divers seeking a seamless and unforgettable experience.

    $1500 including all taxes and fees: accommodations included

    Custom Package

    Create your own personalized diving experience tailored to your schedule, skill level, and interests. Whether it’s a short getaway or an extended trip, we’ll design the perfect package for you.


  3. Provide Your Details: Fill out the booking form with your personal information, diving experience, and any special requirements you may have.

  4. Confirm Your Booking: Review your booking details and confirm your reservation. You will receive a confirmation email with all the necessary information for your diving adventure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our diving packages include guided dives, equipment rental, and access to our experienced dive guides. Some packages may also include training courses and customized diving experiences. Please refer to the specific package details for more information.

No prior diving experience is necessary to book a dive with us. We offer training courses for beginners, and our experienced dive guides will ensure that you have a safe and enjoyable diving experience regardless of your skill level.

Safety is our top priority. Our dive guides are trained in first aid and emergency response, and we follow strict safety protocols during all dives. We also provide high-quality equipment and ensure that all divers are briefed on safety procedures before each dive.
